### Claude

Generated the full business plan for "Prospect Path" (`business-plan.md`), including:

All financial/market figures explicitly labeled as assumptions pending real validation.

### 3. User

> review maxwellpreasha.preasha-home.com for an example website. I also can do video and photography to record games and practices

### Claude

Reviewed the live site and provided feedback:

**Strengths:** clean modern layout, dual-sport hub structure (Basketball/Football sub-profiles), strong hero section (class, school, position, identity statement), third-party validation link (Prep Redzone), consistent brand motif, prominent recruiting-inquiry CTA.

**Gaps:** empty/placeholder film section, no stats/measurables block, non-functional "coming soon" contact info, no schedule section, minimal photo gallery.

**Strategic note:** flagged that in-house video/photography capability is a meaningful business-model upgrade, not just an add-on — it directly closes the empty-film-section gap visible on the sample site, differentiates from competitors who only edit supplied footage, and builds in a natural referral engine via on-site presence at games.

Offered two next steps: (1) update the business plan with a Content Capture service tier, (2) turn Maxwell's site into a documented case study/template spec. Asked which — or both.

### 4. User

### Claude

**Updated `business-plan.md`:**

- Outlined how to use the case study in sales conversations
- Listed next steps to bring Maxwell's own site up to full flagship-example standard
